Who Are We

Internship Success boasts of a professional internship management program designed to profit interns and businesses through empowering the youth with skill sets, addressing skills gaps to secure employment, entrepreneurship opportunities, and enhanced life experiences, while addressing business efficiency, growth, and profit opportunities.


Internship Success is a Professional Internship Placement and Management Company that strives to see all graduates benefit from a well-structured internship program.


We seek to find placements for interns from various levels of tertiary institutions, such as FETs and Universities. We offer a well-developed and well-managed internship program that enables interns to fully participate and engage in workplace environments, not only focusing on their field of study but also understanding the full operations of the business model they will be engaged in. By so doing, our interns become not only qualified personnel but enriched and fortified professionals whose ethos is to become successful, innovative, and quantified team players.

Creating Employable Graduates

Bridging the skills gap between higher education and organised business, One intern at the time.
