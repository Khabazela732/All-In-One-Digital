10th August 2023

Strategic Meeting To Strengthen MOU

On Tuesday the 8th of August the Internship Success’ management, Ehlanzeni TVET College’s Central Office management, Ehlanzeni TVET College’s Mapulaneng Campus management together with a group of interns met to discuss issues affecting Interns in their various workplaces. Key on the agenda was to hear the grievances raised by some interns who felt unhappy in their previous host company.

The meeting discussed key issues which paved way for proper communication between the Interns, Campuses and Internship Success as well as seeking intermediary ways of mitigating any possible challenges in the future.

The meeting was also one of the ways of strengthening the working relationship between the Ehlanzeni TVET College, its associates and Internship Success. Internship success strives to be transparent in all its services both to the educational institutions, host companies, recruited interns as well as prospective interns.
